# Van Bibber Group

*Berkeley Axion Works (van Bibber group)*

Axion dark matter group in the Department of Nuclear Engineering at UC Berkeley, led by Karl van Bibber. Designs and builds microwave cavities and resonators for haloscope searches (HAYSTAC, ALPHA, DMRadio), mines Green Bank Telescope data, and develops gamma-ray instrumentation (GALAXIS).

[Group website](https://axions.berkeley.edu) · Based at [[UC Berkeley]]
