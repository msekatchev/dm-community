*Salemi group (Berkeley Physics and LBNL)*

Axion dark matter group in the Department of Physics at UC Berkeley and the LBNL Physics Division, led by Chiara Salemi (since January 2025). Builds superconducting quantum sensors and lumped-element axion searches (DMRadio, ABRACADABRA), works on BREAD, and develops SQUAT sensors. Part of the Berkeley Axion Works, working closely with the van Bibber group and LBNL's superconducting magnet group.

[Group website](https://physics.berkeley.edu/research-faculty/salemi-group) · Based at [[UC Berkeley]]
