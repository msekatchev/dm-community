*ELECTRON SCATTERING FROM DEUTERIUM AT LARGE MOMENTUM TRANSFER AT 180 DEGREES*

Measures the elastic and inelastic electron scattering from the deuteron at scattering angles around 180 degrees, and elastic electron scattering from the proton. A double-arm spectrometer is assembled to detect scattered electrons and recoil deuterons. Ran for 426 hours.
