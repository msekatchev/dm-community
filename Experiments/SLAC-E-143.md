*MEASUREMENTS OF THE NUCLEON SPIN STRUCTURE IN END STATION A AT SLAC*

Uses high-energy polarized electron beams and a set of ammonia based polarized proton and deuteron targets. Both parallel and perpendicular beam -- target spin orientations are used. Studies the proton and neutron spin structure over the range 0.03 < x < 0.8 at momentum transfers greater than 0.3 (GeV/c)**2. Data analysis in progress (July 96).
