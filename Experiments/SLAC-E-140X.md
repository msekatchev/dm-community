# SLAC-E-140X

*MEASUREMENT OF X, Q**2, AND HYDROGEN-DEUTERIUM DEPENDENCE OF R = (SIGMA/L)/(SIGMA/T)*

Measures the ratio R = (sigma/L)/(sigma/T) and F2 for x between 0.1 and 0.7, and Q**2 between 0.5 and 7.0 (GeV/c)**2. Looks for higher twist contributions beyond the next-to-leading order in QCD and target mass effects. Measures also cross-sections and R in the resonance region. Beams from the Nuclear Physics Injector (NPI) in the SLED and the 'normal mode' were used.
